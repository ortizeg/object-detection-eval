# Summary — 260730-c01

Chunk (c) of 3. Chunks (a) latency and (b) SmolVLM2/registry are merged.

## The finding that reshaped this chunk

While checking the clip-correlation caveat, the test split turned out to be
**3 short video clips, not 94 independent images**: 33 frames from a 5-second
span, 31 from 8 seconds, 30 from 7 seconds. Splits are clip-disjoint, so there
is **no leakage** — but the published CIs resample *frames*, treating 30
near-duplicate frames of one possession as 30 independent observations.

`scripts/run_clustered_bootstrap.py` re-runs the identical paired procedure
resampling **clips**. Drawing 3 clusters from 3 yields only 10 distinct
multisets, so it **enumerates all of them with exact multinomial weights**
rather than sampling — no seed, no Monte Carlo error.

Effect: intervals widen **1.4×–3.9×**; significant adjacent pairs fall
**5/6 → 2/6** (15 of all 21 pairs survive).

- **Collapse to ties:** YOLO26m vs DEIM-M, DEIM-M vs YOLOX-M, YOLOX-M vs
  RF-DETR-M, RF-DETR-M vs RTMDet-M, and the headline **YOLO26m vs YOLOX-M**
  (+0.044, CI [−0.007, +0.074]).
- **Survive:** all comparisons of the top three against RTMDet-M, DAMO-YOLO-M,
  RT-DETRv2-M.
- RTMDet-M vs DAMO-YOLO-M flips *to* significant — its difference is small but
  consistent in every clip, and clustering rewards consistency over magnitude.

## Headline replaced

The old headline called YOLOX-M@800 and YOLO26m@640 "joint-best" — comparing at
**unequal input resolution**, with a latency table beside it measured entirely
at 640, and no YOLOX@800 latency existing anywhere. Replaced with the matched-640
result: the top three are indistinguishable, and **latency and licensing** are
what differentiate them. YOLOX-M is the fastest model measured (5.70 ms) *and*
Apache-2.0; YOLO26m is 0.15 ms slower, AGPL-3.0-only, and the one row whose
weights this repo cannot redistribute.

Deleted from the report and README; struck through and annotated in FORK_PLAN
(a historical plan doc). Reasons: **nothing in this repo computes 30.8 or 48.9**
(there is no default-resize ablation arm), and 72.3 / 71.6 are mAP@**50:95**

resolution change with a preprocessing change. The design principle stays.
