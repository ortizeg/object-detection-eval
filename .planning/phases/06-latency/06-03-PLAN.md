<objective>
Land LAT-02 and resolve LAT-04. `scripts/build_trt_engines.py` is a committed, reader-reproducible wrapper that builds one TensorRT fp16 `.engine` per ONNX via `trtexec --fp16` (subprocess, list-args) and re-runs each engine `--noDataTransfers` to capture `trtexec`'s own GPU-only median/percentile timing — replacing the source repo's ad-hoc shell history. On the rented T4 it runs over the 4 unmodified ONNX (YOLO26m + the 3 DETRs) plus the 3 grafted `*_nms.onnx` from Plan 06-02, and — together with Plan 06-01's `run_latency.py` for the uniform e2e numbers — produces the three small committed results JSONs. LAT-04 is the decision gate over those numbers: if the fair to-boxes fp16 band lands in `[4.0, 7.1]` ms and the on-GPU NMS delta (grafted to-boxes minus model-only) lands in `[0.05, 0.2]` ms, publish from committed code; otherwise the results JSON carries the exact label `manually measured 2026-07-21, not reproducible from this repo`. Both outcomes are passing by design — the honest label is a first-class success, never a silent adjustment of the published numbers.

Purpose: this is the T4-gated wave. The subprocess/JSON logic is written and mock-tested on CPU now; the real build + benchmark + the LAT-04 verdict happen at a single blocking human-verify checkpoint on the rented box, mirroring Plan 05-03's box run. (LAT-02, LAT-04)
Output: `scripts/build_trt_engines.py`, `tests/scripts/test_build_trt_engines.py`, and the three committed results JSONs produced at the checkpoint.
</objective>
